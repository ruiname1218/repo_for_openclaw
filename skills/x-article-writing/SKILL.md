# X Article Writing

## Default objective

Publish one useful, interesting X Article that people want to read/save/share.

## Article style rules

- Prioritize usefulness + curiosity over formal academic tone.
- Keep Japanese natural and readable, not translation-like.
- Start with a strong hook (why this matters now).
- Use short sections and concrete examples.
- End with actionable takeaways.

## Suggested structure

1. Title (clear + curiosity trigger)
2. Hook paragraph (problem/opportunity)
3. 3-5 short sections
4. Practical summary (what to do next)

## Quality checklist

- Topic is timely in AI/tech and relevant now.
- Includes concrete detail (numbers/examples/comparisons) where possible.
- Uses at least 2 credible sources when available.
- Feels like a human writer, concise and sharp.

## Publishing constraints

- Use existing attached X Relay tab only.
- Never open a new X tab.
- If Relay tab is missing/unusable, stop and return reconnect steps.

## Result format after publish

Return:
- article title
- article URL
- 2-3 line summary
